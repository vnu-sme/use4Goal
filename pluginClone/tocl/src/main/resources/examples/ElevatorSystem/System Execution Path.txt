A More Suitable Sequence
Here’s how an elevator typically works:

press() → move() → openDoor() → [addTargetFloor() calls] → closeDoor() → move() → ...

press(): Someone calls the elevator from a floor.
move(): The elevator travels to that floor.
openDoor(): The doors open to let passengers board.
addTargetFloor(): Passengers select their destination floors while the doors are open or shortly after boarding.
closeDoor(): The doors close once everyone is inside and destinations are set.
move(): The elevator moves to the next requested floor.
